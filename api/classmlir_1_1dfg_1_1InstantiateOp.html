Verifies that the instantiated actor exists and has a matching signature.

static ::mlir::ParseResult parse(::mlir::OpAsmParser &parser, ::mlir::OperationState &result)

Parses an InstantiateOp from its custom assembly syntax.

The expected syntax is: 

`dfg.instantiate`
  (`offloaded` (`=` hardware-name)?)?
  @actorName
  (`inputs` `(` operand-list `)`)?
  (`outputs` `(` operand-list `)`)?
  `:` func-type

The optional offloaded keyword enables hardware offloading; appending =name selects a specific backend (default: EmitHLS). The inputs/outputs lists contain pre-existing SSA operands rather than new declarations, and port types are not written inline — they are resolved through the trailing FunctionType. The operandSegmentSizes attribute is derived automatically from the parsed operand counts.
