PICEA Conversion Passes

-convert-affine-to-emithls

Convert Affine to EmitHLS dialect operations.

-convert-arith-to-emithls

Convert Arith to EmitHLS dialect arith operations.

-convert-base2-to-emithls

Convert Base2 to EmitHLS dialect operations.

-convert-dfg-to-emithls

Convert DFG to EmitHLS dialect operations.

-convert-filter-to-linalg

Convert Filter to Linalg named and generic operations

-convert-func-to-dfg

Convert Func to DFG dialect operations.

-convert-index-to-emithls

Convert Index to EmitHLS dialect index operations.

-convert-linalg-to-picea-loops

Convert linalg generic operations to affine loops with PICEA optimizations.

-convert-memref-pad-to-picea-loops

Convert MemRef to EmitHLS dialect operations.

-convert-reshaped-copy-to-loops

Convert a memref copy through a pure reshape or window into an element loop.
